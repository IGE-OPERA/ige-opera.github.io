### Satellite imagery (home page)

Satellite images on the home page are works of NASA (MODIS Land Rapid Response Team, NASA GSFC),
obtained via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) and in the **public domain**
(NASA copyright policy):

- **Atmosphere** — [Saharan Dust over the Mediterranean](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saharan_Dust_over_the_Mediterranean_(MODIS).jpg) (MODIS/Aqua, July 2023)
- **Ocean** — [Phytoplankton bloom in the Barents Sea](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Phytoplankton_bloom_in_the_Barents_Sea_(MODIS_2017-09-14).jpg) (MODIS/Terra, August 2017)

### Climate stripes

The warming stripes strip on the home page is generated by us
(`scripts/make_warming_stripes.py`) from **NASA GISTEMP v4** global annual mean
surface temperature anomalies (public domain data).
