OPERA research group · IGE Grenoble

Open computational earth and climate science

We study the atmosphere, the ocean and the cryosphere in the context of climate change — developing the models, methods and open tools that tomorrow's climate science needs.

Our group replicates the tensions of our community: FORTRAN-based community models on one side, emulators, hybrid models and agentic AI on the other. We are here to experiment with both.
